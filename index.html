The Person Behind the Code

Teaching Philosophy

Mr Sadek believes the best way to learn programming is through demonstration — explaining a concept like a for loop, breaking it down step by step, then showing it both theoretically and practically at the same time.

He takes on a mentoring role, staying available via email even after working hours so students can always reach out for guidance.
